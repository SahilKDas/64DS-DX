# Gate 23 mounted ov102's QUESTION_BLOCK -- its six slots, its state machine

# THE FRAME IS HOST-COPIED, THE CONTENT BODIES ARE NOT. The frame

# pointer-to-member -- they are straight Actor::Spawn wrappers -- so they are
# sliced and linked as matched src, exactly as-is.
